# Template Setup Guide

This is a generic template for professional accounting and taxation services. To customize it for your business, please replace the following placeholders throughout the codebase:

## Required Replacements

### Company Information
- `[Your Company Name]` → Your actual company name
- `[Your Company]` → Short version of your company name
- `[X]` → Number of years in business
- `Your Company Name` → Your actual company name (in HTML title)

### Contact Information
- `[Your Business Address], [City], [State/Province] [Postal Code]` → Your complete business address
- `[+1 (555) 123-4567]` → Your phone number
- `[contact@yourcompany.com]` → Your email address
- `https://wa.me/15551234567` → Your WhatsApp number (in WhatsAppWidget.tsx)

### Location Services
- Google Maps iframe → Update the src URL to point to your actual business location

### Client Testimonials
- `[Client Name]` → Actual client names (with permission)
- `[Client Company]` → Actual client company names
- Update testimonial quotes to reflect real feedback

### Additional Customizations
- Update social media links in Footer.tsx (currently placeholder links)
- Customize services in Services.tsx to match your offerings
- Update the professional team image in About.tsx
- Modify color scheme in index.html if desired (currently uses navy and gold theme)

## Files to Update
1. `index.html` - Page title
2. `components/Header.tsx` - Company name in navigation
3. `components/About.tsx` - Company description and years of experience
4. `components/Contact.tsx` - All contact information and map location
5. `components/Footer.tsx` - Company name and contact details
6. `components/WhatsAppWidget.tsx` - WhatsApp phone number
7. `components/Testimonials.tsx` - Client testimonials and names
8. `README.md` - Documentation (already partially updated)

## Optional Customizations
- Modify services in `components/Services.tsx`
- Update styling colors in `index.html`
- Replace the professional team image in `components/About.tsx`
- Add your actual social media links in `components/Footer.tsx`

Happy customizing! 🚀
